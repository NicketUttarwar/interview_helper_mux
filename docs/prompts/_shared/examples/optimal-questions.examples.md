# optimal-questions examples (reference)

**Good** (missing_question, ≤15 words): "What pushed you to pivot the product in 2024?"

**Bad** (leading): "You must have been terrified during the layoffs, right?"

**Bad** (too long): "Can you walk me through the entire history of how your co-founder relationship evolved from the beginning through today?"

**Bad** (puts words in mouth): "So you basically gave up on enterprise sales."

**Bad** (interruptive after mic-drop prior): Prior native close = “cash in our Bham account was higher than cash in our Mumbai account.” → “Let me pause you there — what was the turning point…?”

**Good** (courteous after same prior): “Given what you just said — how did that reshape what came next?” (uses `prior_native_contexts`; never aims at micro “Okay.”)

v1: always `delivery: record` — never use `synthesize`.
