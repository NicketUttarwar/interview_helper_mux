# Podcast show description — good vs bad examples

Flow 3 stage: `podcast_show_description` · Prompt: [podcast-show-description.system.txt](../../publishing/podcast-show-description.system.txt)

---

## Good (third person, ~200 words, evidence-grounded)

> **Hook + stakes:** A founder who walked away from a nine-figure acquisition explains why the deal died in the final week — and what the board never heard.
>
> In this conversation, the interviewee traces how product-market fit masked a structural channel conflict until diligence surfaced it. The thread moves from early scrappy distribution through the term sheet, the break, and the rebuild: a smaller team, a narrower ICP, and a pricing model built around usage instead of seats. Along the way, the discussion touches hiring after a near-exit, the cost of optimism in board updates, and why "default alive" became an operating principle rather than a slogan.
>
> Listeners who follow B2B SaaS, M&A, or founder psychology will hear a candid post-mortem without victory-lap framing — including what the interviewee would repeat and what they would not.

**Why it works:** Third person throughout; specific arc; no invented numbers; invites a defined audience; length in band.

---

## Bad — first person / second person

> We sat down with Jane to hear her incredible journey. You'll learn how she built a rocket ship and why you need to listen if you're a founder.

**Fix:** Rewrite as third person; replace "you'll learn" with "Listeners hear…" or "The episode explores…".

---

## Bad — generic hype without evidence

> An insightful deep dive into leadership, innovation, and the future of technology. The guest shares powerful lessons every entrepreneur needs.

**Fix:** Name concrete topics, stakes, and outcomes from `content_brief` / segments; drop empty adjectives.

---

## Bad — transcript dump

> "So what happened was we were at about four million ARR and then the VP of sales said…" [400 words of quoted dialogue]

**Fix:** Paraphrase; keep quotes under ~15 words or omit.

---

## Bad — too short

> Great chat about startups. Worth a listen.

**Fix:** Expand to 150+ words with hook, themes, and audience pitch per schema.
