## Synthetic VO register (topic-forward)

Honor this register for every spoken `text` field in body layups, transitions, bridges, and adjudicate rewrites. Planner fields (`target_beat`, `forward_unlock`, `listener_need_entering_T`, `next_beat_card`, `episode_card`, rationales) stay analysis-only — never paste them into air. When `next_beat_card` or `episode_card` is in the input, match the spoken line's diction to that description. Do not add a JSON key for the card.

### Topic-forward default

- Orient with **topics, mechanisms, stakes, places, products, and numbers** — not who is about to speak.
- **Do not name interview participants** (host, guest, founder, CEO-as-person) unless a proper noun is the fact itself (company, drug, city, product).
- **Never use role labels on air:** "the host", "the guest", "the interviewer", "our speaker", "on the show today".
- **No gendered pronouns** (he / she / him / her); use **they** or rephrase without a pronoun.
- **At most one person-proper-noun per line**; never repeat the same name twice in one segment.
- Manners stay **benign**. Diction follows `next_beat_card` or `episode_card` for this seam when that card is in the input. Avoid editorializing or "making a point."

### Attribution patterns

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| "Let's hear **X** explain…" / naming who speaks next | Topic-first setup: "The next piece is cell biopsy — CTCs, not fragments alone." |
| "**Name** explains… **Name** now…" | "Cell biopsy changes what blood-only assays can miss." |
| "The **host** now explains…" | "Next: what limits ctDNA-only analysis." |
| "He closes with…" | "That closes with…" / "The access goal behind it is…" |

### Creative quality bar

- **Vary sentence openings** across the episode — no template rhythm ("X explains… X now describes…").
- Lead with a **concrete grounded hook** (number, contrast, causal pivot) from nuggets or brief.
- Endings are **free-form** — a forward unlock into the next beat is optional. Never require "Let's hear…" / "what happened next" closers.
- Prefer **active topic verbs** (pivot, hinge, trade-off, bottleneck) over attribution verbs (explains, says, elaborates) tied to a person.

### Narrow exception

- **`episode_preface` / cold open only:** orient *who / what / why* from `episode_card` when it is present (thesis, domain, stakes; guest proper noun **once** when the brief supplies one). End when orientation is complete — no mandatory hinge or quiz. Ban abstract closers (“What does X ask/reveal/mean…?”). Never "the host." The card stays analysis-only.
