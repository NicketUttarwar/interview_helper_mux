Write a podcast **show description** (episode blurb) for distribution — Flow 3 (see task in volley).

The description should **allure and entice** listeners to consume the full episode. It is marketing copy for podcast apps, websites, and social previews — not a neutral summary.

## Voice and length

- **Third person only** — refer to the interviewer and interviewee by role or name from `speakers` / profile; never use "we", "our", "you" (listener), or "I".
- Target **180–220 words** (hard bounds: **150–250**). One to three short paragraphs; no bullet lists unless the platform requires them (default: prose paragraphs).
- Present tense or light past tense for narrative momentum; avoid passive filler ("In this episode, topics are discussed").

## Content requirements

Ground every claim in supplied evidence (`content_brief`, `analysis_state`, segment samples, `major_questions`). You may **frame** and **emphasize**; you may not invent facts, quotes, metrics, or guest credentials.

**Evidence anchors (required — validated post-stage):**
- Populate `evidence_segment_ids` with real `segment_id` values from the manifest.
- Every `themes_highlighted` entry and each `content_brief.key_claims[]` item must be supportable via those segments or explicit prose in `description_markdown`.
- `hook_sentence` must match the opening of `description_markdown`.
- `word_count` must match the markdown body (±15 words tolerance).

Include, when supported by evidence:

1. **Hook** — the single most compelling tension, surprise, or payoff (first 1–2 sentences).
2. **Stakes** — why this conversation matters to `content_brief.audience` or profile audience.
3. **Thread** — 2–4 concrete themes or questions the episode explores (woven into prose, not a dry list).
4. **Guest / interviewee angle** — what perspective they bring (role, domain, story) without résumé padding.
5. **Close** — a forward-looking line that invites listening (still third person; no "Subscribe now").

Use `emotional_beats` and `key_claims` for color; prefer **specific** nouns and outcomes over generic podcast adjectives ("insightful", "deep dive") unless paired with substance.

## Do not

- Write in first or second person.
- Paste transcript quotes longer than ~15 words (paraphrase instead).
- Spoil every beat or timestamp the episode.
- Claim audio/SFX/mix qualities the listener cannot verify from text.
- Mention internal pipeline terms (Flow 1, EDL, gap report, STT, etc.).

## Memory sync

- `narrative_patch.audience` — only if the blurb sharpens audience positioning vs prior profile
- `confidence_patch.show_description`: 0–1 for how well evidence supported the copy

## Investigations

- `show_description_thin_evidence` — if thesis or themes are weak after reading volley; suggest `content_context` or profile verification rerun
