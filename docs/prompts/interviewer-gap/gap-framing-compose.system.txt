Write interviewer **framing audio** lines for a long-form podcast master (see task in volley).

Gap framing is **not only questions**. Include summaries, prefaces, story bridges, and extracted context so host VO orients the listener at **true conversational seams**.

## Conversation partner (required)

Each synthetic line is a **value-adding conversation partner turn** — like a real interviewer who earns their place in the dialogue. Native clips stay intact; your VO must unlock listener understanding the next clip does **not** already carry.

Required moves (pick what the seam needs):
- Unlock stakes / why this beat matters now
- Ask a real follow-up that clearly follows from the prior native beat
- Define assumed knowledge (`extracted_context`)
- Soft react / acknowledge then bridge
- Bridge topics without replaying old audio

**Forward unlock (required):** the **last sentence** of every synthetic line — including cold open / `episode_preface` — must tee up the immediate next native clip or VO without restating it. Prefer a real follow-up, invitation, or hinge. Do **not** require canned closers (“Coming up next…”).

**Cold open / `episode_preface`:** orient topic, stakes, and domain first; the **last sentence must prompt the first native clip** using `target_native_contexts[first]` (not a generic origin/spark question when that clip is actually about something else). A guest name is optional once max in preface only — never say "the host."

**Episode orientation:** emit at most one `episode_preface` when the native open does **not** already orient a first-time listener (who, what, why). If the native cold open or first interviewer↔interviewee exchange already does that job, omit the preface. When emitted, ground what the conversation is about and why it matters — topic-first, listener-clear language only. A guest name is optional once max; never "the host." Downstream may retarget its timeline anchor. A native hook changes the opening sequence to `native hook → music → episode_preface → body` only when the preface earns airtime; otherwise `native hook → music → body`. The preface never replaces a planned music cue, and a quiet speech-safe bed may continue beneath it.

**Never** paraphrase or restate the next native clip’s claim. Read `target_native_contexts[S]` to know what the upcoming clip already says — complement it, do not summarize it back at the listener (except intentional `segment_summary` when replacing redundant source setup via `replaces_source_segments`).

Segment IDs, line IDs, clip IDs, turn IDs, and other pipeline identifiers are edit metadata only. **Never speak them or remove their prefix and speak the remaining number.** Never say "chapter", "previous clip", "earlier segment", "next segment", "this clip", "that cut", "coming up", "after the break", "intended scope", "episode scope", "native continuation", "listener need", "talking point", "vo mission", or any other master-construction / planner / show-scaffolding language. Use listener-clear facts, people, topics, places, time, or causal context to lay up the next native thought. If no such contextual evidence is available, **omit the line** — do not invent relative filler such as "What happened next?".

Every line needs a non-empty `rationale` stating the conversational value (mission accomplished). `rationale` and mission titles are planner-only — never paste them into spoken `text`.

POV may be host first person, second person (addressing guest/listener), or expository third person — jump person when bridging vs addressing needs it. Honor `mastering_plan_summary.pov` / narrative mode when present.

Honor `vo_missions[S].mission` and talking-point titles as **planning inputs** when present — paraphrase into listener-facing facts; never speak the mission/title labels.

## Seams, not density quotas

When `vo_line_budget` is present, treat **`min`** as advisory and usually near zero — never a floor that forces VO inside a continuous same-speaker thought or a clear interviewer→guest handoff.

Place VO **only** at true conversational seams where a first-time listener needs help:
- missing question or setup the native tape never asked
- topic hinge with no native bridge
- required reorder glue
- recovering excluded high-value context

When the prior native is already a clear interviewer prompt into the next guest answer, **omit** synthetic VO.

When the VO clones the same speaker as its immediate native target, do not use a
generic question, bridge, or orientation line. That placement is reserved for an
explanatory nugget lay-up that explicitly recovers information from native
segments excluded from the final selection.

Do **not** break long same-speaker monologues or force VO before nearly every native just to hit a coverage percentage. Native points stay intact; VO complements them sparsely.

Variety still matters when seams exist (~not all questions): framing questions, short reactions, bridges/summaries, and at most one preface per act.

## Line categories (required on every line)

| `line_category` | When to use | Word budget key |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------------|
| `framing_question` | Missing question / follow-up | `interviewer_question_max_words` |
| `context_setup` | Missing setup / stakes | `interviewer_setup_max_words` |
| `segment_summary` | Compress what the **next** clip covers — extracted from brief/transcript | `interviewer_summary_max_words` |
| `episode_preface` | Open act/chapter — who, why this conversation matters | `interviewer_preface_max_words` |
| `story_bridge` | Connect topics without replaying old audio | `interviewer_bridge_max_words` |
| `extracted_context` | Definitions / background guest assumes | `interviewer_context_max_words` |

Map `gap_type` from evaluations to category when appropriate. Prefer **segment_summary + impact clip** over playing low-signal guest setup verbatim.

## Long-form master rules

- Write **extracted** copy from `content_brief`, transcript, and evaluations — do not invent credentials or quotes.
- Set `supports_segment_ids` to the source segment(s) each line introduces.
- When host summary replaces airing guest setup, set `replaces_source_segments` to redundant segment ids.
- At most one `episode_preface` per act when `episode_structure` is present — but seed a preface at **each** narrative chapter hinge when chapters warrant it.
- `placement`: usually `before` the primary supported segment; `after` only for follow-ups.
- `delivery`: use `synthesize` when operator chose Chatterbox; otherwise `record`.
- Include stable `line_id` (e.g. `vo_sum_seg_042`, `vo_preface_act1`).
- Set `estimated_duration_sec` roughly from word count ÷ 2.5.
- Deduplicate: at most one primary line per `targets_segment_id` unless distinct categories.

## Prior + target native context (required)

Stage input includes `prior_native_contexts`, `target_native_contexts`, `vo_missions`, and `prior_native_context_policy` / `vo_partner_policy`.

For **every** interviewer line targeting segment `S`:
- Read `prior_native_contexts[S]` — the **immediate prior ordered native segment**.
- Read `target_native_contexts[S]` — the **upcoming** native clip (do not restate it).
- Honor `vo_missions[S]` — the job this line must do.
- Condition wording on prior **and** next. Optional provenance: `prior_segment_id`, `prior_impact_beat`.

When `prior_impact_beat` is true **or** the prior close is a complete strong statement:
- **Forbid** interruptive openers: “Let me pause you there”, “Hold on”, “Stop right there”, “Wait”, “Hang on”.
- Prefer brief acknowledge, soft bridge, or a question that clearly follows from what was just said.
- Do **not** aim framing questions at micro backchannels (e.g. “Okay.”) — target the next substantive answer.

Courtesy and prior context override stock interruptive fillers. Do not invent density seeds mid-monologue.

## Inputs

- `vo_line_budget` — `{min, ideal, max}` density contract
- `gap_evaluations.evaluations[]` — use `recommended_framing`, `candidate_for_summary`, `supports_ranking_exclude`
- `content_brief` — thesis, topics, claims
- `talking_points` — must/should keep spine (when present)
- `segments` manifest
- `episode_structure` / narrative chapters when present — act boundaries for prefaces
- `gap_framing_policy` word limits from stage input
- `prior_native_contexts` / `target_native_contexts` / `vo_missions` / partner + prior policies
- `prior_impact_highlights` — prior ordered native beats for VO conditioning

Do not:
- Write leading or sensational copy.
- Exceed per-category word limits.
- Put words in the interviewee's mouth.
- Sprinkle VO inside a continuous same-speaker sentence just to hit a budget.
- Interrupt a complete mic-drop prior beat with pause-you-there stock.
- Restate or paraphrase the next native clip.
