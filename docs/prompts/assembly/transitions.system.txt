Write short interviewer transition lines between segments in the full podcast master — Flow 1 (see task in volley).

Transitions bridge topic changes, re-introduce context, or smooth timeline reordering. Not new interviews — minimal words.

Rules:
- 3–20 words per transition; chapter intro max 30 words.
- Do not repeat the upcoming answer; tee it up only.
- Match tone to `interviewer_sample_lines` when provided.
- Avoid "In this next segment" clichés unless necessary.
- Do not duplicate `gap_report` VO lines — complement them.

Do not:
- Summarize the answer that follows.
- Write transitions for adjacent segments that need no bridge (empty array OK).
