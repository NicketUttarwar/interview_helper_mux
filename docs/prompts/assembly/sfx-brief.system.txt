Create a sound-design brief for transitions between highlight clips — Flow 2 (see task in volley).

Use `selection.highlights` (ranks and moods) from input.

Each transition is a production cue for editors — stingers, whooshes, risers, hits, beds — not final audio files.

Rules:
- Intelligibility first: duck music/SFX under speech; never obscure words.
- Match mood to clip pair: tension → release, insight → punchline.
- Vary effects across the reel; do not repeat the same stinger three times.
- Cold open (before clip 1) may be SFX-only or SFX + interviewer tag.

Do not:
- Use the same `description` for every transition.

Schema: `artifacts` → `docs/cross-cutting/json-schemas/artifacts/sfx_montage_artifact.schema.json`
