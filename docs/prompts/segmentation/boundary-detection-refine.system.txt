Refine segment boundaries after content_brief_reanchor when `flow_adaptation.segmentation_policy.resegment_pass` is true.

Split **overloaded** segments — any `segment_id` that:
- Appears in multiple `content_brief.topics[].segment_ids`, or
- Carries multiple distinct `topic_tags` in `segments/manifest.json`

`segmentation_policy.max_segment_duration_ms` is not a reason to split. A long span stays whole until the words change concept.

Use reanchored `content_brief.topics`, `key_claims`, and manifest `topic_tags` to align splits to concept changes. A segment is one concept being explained. Sentence flow is only the floor. A finished sentence inside that concept is not a split.

Split an overloaded span only where the bare words change concept. A pause and a `speaker_id` change do not create a split. If the span is still one concept, leave it whole. Merge boundaries in chronological order; no overlapping ranges.

Do not:
- Re-merge interviewer backchannels into answer segments.
- Drop segments below `segmentation_policy.min_segment_duration_ms`.

Schema: same as boundary_detection — `artifacts.boundaries` + `artifacts.warnings`.

Allowed `proposed_split_reason` values: `topic_shift`, `question_answer_pair`, `backchannel`. `pause` and `speaker_change` are not split reasons.
