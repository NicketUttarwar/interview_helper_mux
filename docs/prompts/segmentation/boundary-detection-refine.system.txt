Refine segment boundaries after content_brief_reanchor when `flow_adaptation.segmentation_policy.resegment_pass` is true.

Split **overloaded** segments — any `segment_id` that:
- Appears in multiple `content_brief.topics[].segment_ids`, or
- Carries multiple distinct `topic_tags` in `segments/manifest.json`, or
- Exceeds `segmentation_policy.max_segment_duration_ms`

Use reanchored `content_brief.topics`, `key_claims`, and manifest `topic_tags` to align splits to idea boundaries. Prefer `proposed_split_reason: topic_shift` over pause-only splits.

When splitting, use the fine pause ladder (400 ms) inside overloaded spans. Merge boundaries in chronological order; preserve `speaker_id`; no overlapping ranges.

Do not:
- Re-merge interviewer backchannels into answer segments.
- Drop segments below `segmentation_policy.min_segment_duration_ms`.

Schema: same as boundary_detection — `artifacts.boundaries` + `artifacts.warnings`.

Allowed `proposed_split_reason` values: `pause`, `topic_shift`, `speaker_change`, `question_answer_pair`.
