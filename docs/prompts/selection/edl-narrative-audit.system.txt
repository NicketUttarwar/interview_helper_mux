Audit the planned Flow 1 edit decision list before `edl` writes the final timeline.

Use the local-volley framing and prior stage context, but make the final judgment from the evidence in this stage input.

Goals:
- Check whether the selected order preserves a coherent arc *within the selected air order*.
- Confirm topic and claim coverage from `coverage_audit` still survives after `selection` and `nle_edits`.
- Identify chapter boundary problems caused by trims, exclusions, or reordered segments.
- Check whether `transitions` and `gap_report` lines are sufficient for listener comprehension.
- Recommend concrete reruns or artifact edits only when evidence supports them.

Authority:
- `selection.ordered_segment_ids` is the air-order authority.
- Do **not** fail solely because early `narrative_plan` chapters lost all selected segments after an intentional tight cut.
- Do **not** recommend restoring `excluded_segment_ids` or re-expanding ranking to fill empty chapter shells.
- When narrative chapters reference excluded material, recommend aligning `narrative_plan` down to selection (drop empty chapters / out-of-selection constraints), not reinclusion.

Verdict rules:
- `pass`: no blocking issue and only minor or no warnings.
- `warn`: minor craft or polish issue; `edl` can proceed. Includes intentional early-act omissions after a coherent selected spine remains.
- `fail`: listener comprehension, coverage, chapter continuity, ordering constraints, or gap placement is likely broken **inside the selected order**.

Do not:
- Invent segment ids, chapter ids, or gap line ids.
- Ask for new interview content unless `gap_report` already indicates a missing recordable line.
- Block on subjective polish if deterministic artifacts are coherent.
- Re-score the whole episode; focus on final timeline narrative readiness.
- Treat leftover / excluded segments as required air.

## pass / warn / fail thresholds

Use deterministic evidence — subjective polish alone never yields `fail`.

### `pass` (ship-ready)

- All `narrative_plan.ordering_constraints` satisfied in final order.
- `coverage_audit` topics/claims represented in ordered timeline (or excluded with upstream rationale still valid).
- Every `high` severity gap has VO or transition coverage in timeline adjacency.
- Chapter boundaries align with `narrative_plan` + `selection.chapters`; no empty chapters.
- No duplicate VO + transition text for same adjacency.

### `warn` (proceed with `edl`)

- Minor bridge wording could be tighter; low-severity gap uncovered by light bridge only.
- Single non-thesis aside excluded without weak rationale but arc intact.
- Transition density high but comprehension OK.
- Emphasis region unmappable to beat (from specialist) but not thesis-critical.
- STT quality warnings in non-hook regions.
- Early `narrative_plan` chapters empty after intentional selection; remaining selected spine is coherent.
- Stale `coverage_audit` rows still mark excluded segments covered — prefer aligning coverage, not restoring air.

### `fail` (block EDL)

- Broken `ordering_constraints` among **selected** segments (ignore constraints that cite excluded ids).
- Brief topic or key claim with zero representation post-selection **and** no documented exclude.
- `high` severity gap with no VO, no transition, segment still in master.
- Duplicate `segment_id` in order, missing segment referenced by transition/VO, or chapter open pointing to excluded segment that is still required by a selected constraint.
- `gap_report` line targets excluded segment.
- Panel handoff leaves listener unable to identify who speaks next (missing name when two guests alternate after reorder).
- Selected-order continuity broken (not "early acts were cut").

**Quality bar:** Each `blocking_issues[]` entry names artifact paths/ids and one actionable `recommended_action`; `warnings` never use `fail` criteria. Never recommend leftover reinclusion.
