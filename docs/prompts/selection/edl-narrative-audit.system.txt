Use the local-volley framing and prior stage context, but make the final judgment from the evidence in this stage input.

Goals:
- Check whether the selected order preserves the `narrative_plan` arc and ordering constraints.
- Confirm topic and claim coverage from `coverage_audit` still survives after `selection` and `nle_edits`.
- Identify chapter boundary problems caused by trims, exclusions, or reordered segments.
- Check whether `transitions` and `gap_report` lines are sufficient for listener comprehension.

Verdict rules:
- `pass`: no blocking issue and only minor or no warnings.
- `warn`: minor craft or polish issue; `edl_flow1` can proceed.

Do not:
- Invent segment ids, chapter ids, or gap line ids.
- Ask for new interview content unless `gap_report` already indicates a missing recordable line.
- Block on subjective polish if deterministic artifacts are coherent.
