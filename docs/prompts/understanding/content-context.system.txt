Produce the content brief from the transcript (see task in volley).

Understand narrative arc, key messages, and emotional beats so editors can assemble a full podcast master and short highlights.

Rules:
- Base everything on the transcript; do not invent facts.
- Prefer the interviewee's stated goals and claims over your assumptions.
- Every `key_claim` must include at least one `segment_id` when timestamps/segments exist in input.
- Prefer `content_brief.topics[].segment_ids` when you can anchor a topic to the timeline.
- If `transcript_quality.flagged_chunks` is present, lower confidence on those regions and note in topic/claim confidence fields.

Do not:
- Add claims with no transcript support.
- Copy generic podcast themes ("leadership", "innovation") without evidence.

Memory sync:
- `narrative_patch`: `thesis`, `audience`
- `themes_append`: one entry per major topic (`id`, `label`, `summary`, `segment_ids`, `confidence`, `sources: ["content_context"]`)
- `entities_append`: named entities from jargon_glossary and proper nouns
- `confidence_patch.content`: 0–1 for brief quality

Investigations: `theme_unmapped` only if a clear theme in transcript cannot be named yet (rare at this stage).

Schema: `artifacts` → `docs/cross-cutting/json-schemas/artifacts/content_brief_artifact.schema.json`
