Propose ideal native cut windows for each talking point using the timed transcript.

Inputs include `talking_points`, transcript words with timestamps, and the content brief. Your job is to choose the **best native spans** that prove each talking point — not to pause-split the whole tape.

Rules:
- Every `must_keep` / `should_keep` talking point needs at least one cut (more only when distinctly different beats are required).
- `start_ms` / `end_ms` must land on real transcript **word timestamps**. Prefer the best **conceptual / listen-complete idea hinge** for the talking point — cut **anywhere** a word ends a listen-complete idea unit. Full grammatical sentences (`.!?`) are one valid hinge type, not the only one. Never mid-word.
- Speakers ramble and chain ideas with “and…and…so…”. Do **not** wait for a period if a stronger idea hinge (payoff close, topic turn, answer end) exists earlier. Do **not** keep trailing digression after the payoff.
- Explicitly **forbid** hanging setups / mid-promise chops as `end_ms` — never end on tails like “that is the time”, “the fact that”, “in terms of”, “the reason why”, “how do we”, or trailing “and/that/the + noun” setups that still promise continuation. When the idea needs more tape, extend `end_ms` to the completing conceptual close — not a convenient pause mid-setup.
- Prefer preserving speaker volleys (question+answer) intact when both are needed for comprehension.
- Avoid redundant cuts that repeat the same idea.
- `priority` should match the talking point importance unless you demote a weak native proof to `optional` and warn.
- `rationale` must say why *this* window is the ideal proof (not "because there was a pause").
- Do not invent dialogue; windows must contain the claimed evidence.
- Sort cuts by `start_ms`. Avoid heavy overlaps; if two must_keeps collide, keep the stronger proof and warn.
- **Distribute cuts across the full interview timeline.** Do not cluster all proofs in the cold open or first few minutes. On long interviews, must_keep talking points that appear mid/late in the transcript must have cuts in those later regions. The span from earliest cut `start_ms` to latest cut `end_ms` must cover **at least ~45%** of the source duration (earliest→latest), with proofs in early, middle, and late thirds when the transcript supports them.

Schema: `artifacts` → ideal_cuts_artifact.schema.json

Place the stage artifact inside the required envelope as `artifacts` with:
- `cuts` (array)
- `coverage_notes` (string, optional)
- `warnings` (array, optional)
