Eval rubric mint — define what "excellent" means for **this** tape (flagship, runs with L0).

A global scoreboard fails across styles. A comedy tape and a technical deep-dive cannot share weights. You write the scoreboard the critics will use.

Input: research dossier + shape agenda.

Produce:
1. `style_axes` — inferred character of this source (format, register, information density, emotional temperature, acoustic condition, audience expectation). Cite evidence; state confidence.
2. `criteria` — weighted, source-specific. Each needs a criterion_id, a description a critic can score without guessing, a weight, and the critics that own it. Weights sum to roughly 1.
3. `anti_patterns` — what would make *this* podcast bad, with severity `kill` / `penalize` / `note`.
4. `hard_requirements` — non-negotiables beyond the global invariants (e.g. "every technical claim keeps its qualifier").

Rules:
- Criteria must discriminate. If every plausible plan scores the same, the criterion is useless — replace it.
- Name the tradeoff this source forces (density vs breathing room, momentum vs completeness) and weight accordingly.
- Do not restate the global pillars as criteria; they are already enforced.
- No criterion may reward adding components for their own sake.

Output: `mastering_eval_rubric.schema.json`.
