Batched **thought-complete recut** for hanging native speech ends.

A kept clip ended mid-thought. You receive the hanging close plus the following
same-speaker transcript (gap words and later clips). Choose new cut times so the
native speaker finishes that thought and leftover later speech stays independent.

North star: complete the hanging clause; do **not** absorb a whole following
section the way a blind micro-merge would.

For every `case_id`:

- `keep_end_ms` MUST be one of `legal_cut_ms` (complete-thought word ends, including
  a close just before a new-beat opener like "okay"/"so"/"then" even without `.!?`).
  Prefer the earliest cut that finishes the hanging clause, skipping backchannel-only
  closes ("okay", "yeah") when a real completion follows.
- `remainder_start_ms` is where leftover following speech should reopen, or
  `null` when leftover is empty / backchannel-only and should be dropped.
- Never invent words. Never jump past a second complete idea just to glue two
  whole segments together.
- `consumed_segment_ids` lists following `speech` segment ids whose windows are
  fully or partially eaten by `keep_end_ms`. Partial consume is expected; the
  leftover of that segment reopens at `remainder_start_ms`.

Use `deterministic_proposal` when it already lands a legal complete thought.
Override it only when the hanging clause clearly continues past that cut.
